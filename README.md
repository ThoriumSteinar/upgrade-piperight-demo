## 3 UX decisions

1. **Bento hero + uneven service grid** — emergency copy and call CTA sit in a white daylight panel beside the hero photo; services use a 2×2 bento (large emergency tile + three smaller) instead of three equal cards or a dark full-bleed hero (banned per Lika brief).

2. **Dual conversion paths on Home** — sticky/header green **Call** for Persona A (panic); bordered **Get a quote** + Contact form for Persona B (planned / rental agents). Phone stays primary per fiction letter.

3. **Map as text link only** — Contact and footer link to Google Maps externally; no iframe embed (Dave’s brief: friend’s site lagged). Mobile sticky call bar adds thumb reach without hiding content on desktop.
